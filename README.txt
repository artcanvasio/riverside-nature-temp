Riverside Nature – temporary standalone copy

Upload this whole folder as its own folder on your server, e.g.
  https://your-domain.com/riverside-nature-temp/
Keep it outside every other project. It is fully self-contained:
nothing in here refers to other files on the server, and nothing else needs to change.

It must be served over https, otherwise phones will not allow the motion sensor.
On the phone: open the link, tap "Pano", allow motion access, then turn the phone.

To remove it later, simply delete the riverside-nature-temp folder.
